Repost filter and original language

Native reposts are excluded, including self-reposts. A post must have a matching top-level permalink author, account username and timeline-item author. Replies are included. Quotes are included only when the account adds its own content. Ambiguous records are excluded. No translations are collected or created.

Account activity does not establish that the named person personally typed each post. Staff may operate an account.

Content display

Original post text is displayed through the platform’s official embed. Scraped post bodies, translations, images and editorial summaries are not republished in this repository. This index retains factual post IDs, dates, timestamps where available, and original permalinks.

Third-party dataset licenses do not by themselves establish rights in a person’s post text. A dataset’s license label is not presented as a license for the underlying social posts. Original content and availability remain controlled by the original platform and author.

Time and dates

X creation times are derived from the exact decimal Snowflake post ID. Older IDs retain an available timezone-aware source timestamp. Month/day navigation uses UTC dates for exact timestamps. University records with only a calendar date keep that date; their clock time is marked unavailable and is never inferred.

All IDs remain strings. Records are deduplicated by platform identity or original permalink. No simulated quotes or inferred publication times are added.

Limits

  • This is a snapshot, not a complete account export or live monitor.
  • Uncollected posts, replies, deleted posts and prior revisions may be absent.
  • Embeds may be unavailable after deletion, privacy changes, regional limits or platform sign-in requirements. The original permalink remains available.
  • No login credentials or private-account content were collected.
